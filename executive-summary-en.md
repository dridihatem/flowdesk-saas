## What we do

**FlowDesk** is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform that gives each service company a **branded digital workspace** — from first web lead to paid invoice. One product replaces CRM, project management, quoting, invoicing, client portal, and referral-partner commissions.

Each customer runs on their own subdomain (`company.flowdesk.com`) with **Arabic and English** UI, custom branding, and role-based access for staff, clients, and business providers.

---

## Problem

GCC service SMEs (agencies, consultancies, broker networks) lose time and revenue managing **5–15 disconnected tools**. Global software is expensive, English-first, and missing **client transparency** and **partner commission** workflows common in Gulf markets.

---

## Solution

| Stage | FlowDesk capability |
|---|---|
| Acquire | Embeddable forms, lead inbox |
| Deliver | Projects, tasks, kanban, client comments |
| Sell | Quotes, negotiation, PDF |
| Collect | Invoices, VAT, online payment, reminders |
| Retain | Client portal, installments |
| Partner | Signed provider contracts, commissions, payouts |

**Status:** Advanced MVP live — multi-tenant, subscriptions, Stripe billing, 4 languages (incl. Arabic RTL), provider & client portals.
